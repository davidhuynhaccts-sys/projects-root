# Super Elly: Bakery Monsters, Level 1

Routes: `/superelly` (series landing page), `/superelly/bakery-monsters` (game).

Phaser 3.90 + TypeScript, lazy-loaded into the existing Next.js application. No new backend, authentication, or database. Best cookie score is stored locally. PWA scope and caches are limited to Super Elly. Only Level 1 is playable.

## Play

Touch: four-way movement, separate Jump, hold Flashlight, tap Action for a random cartwheel/forward roll/back bridge, Special when charged. Keyboard: arrows/WASD, Space, F, E, Q; Escape pauses. Action near the machine switch finishes the level after defeating the boss.

Five hearts, brief protection after damage. Monsters move along the floor vertically as well as horizontally and visibly recoil/rotate/fall on hits. Boss throws randomly selected eggs, milk and cheese toward Elly's position after a 750ms warning, with EGG ALLERGY! / DAIRY ALLERGY! comic text. The special is a glowing transformation followed by three combo hits and a Cookie Comet finisher.

Cookies increase the score and add 5 charge each. Monster hits also add charge. Two short, retryable cookie clues teach that unnecessary transportation breaks cookies and a short, careful route reduces waste. Each correct answer gives 20 charge. Combat pauses during the clues.

Four different watercolor rooms, shop, pantry, prep kitchen and machine room, form one 4,400-unit world. Background art is not tiled. Intended first-run duration is 1–2 minutes including reading and exploration; the automated input-driven run takes about 40 active seconds.

## Art and audio

Visual references: the supplied approved game mockup and the sketches in `Elly and the Bakery Monsters 220404(1).pdf`. Elly retains the skeleton costume, ponytail, bare feet and flashlight. Boss follows the first machine-monster sketch, with horns, whisk and measuring cup. Watercolor illustration atlases were generated from the references, cropped into sprites and optimized as WebP. All backgrounds and sprites total about 1.2MB.

Original 108 BPM, eight-bar looping bakery score and ten sound effects are generated by `scripts/make-superelly-audio.py` (numpy + ffmpeg). No external music samples. Music starts from the Play gesture; effects use decoded WebAudio buffers. The header sound control supports mute and retry after a blocked audio start. Music pauses during lessons and when backgrounded. The scoped service worker caches music/effects and supports audio byte ranges.

## Development and verification

`npm ci`, `npm run dev`, `npm run build`. Run `node scripts/test-superelly.cjs` for deterministic model tests and a complete run through both lessons, all six monsters, the boss, and machine completion. Tests also cover vertical movement, jump, five hearts, pause, collection, all acrobatic moves, knockback, all three boss ingredients, warning time, Special and loss/reset.

- Simulation: `app/superelly/bakery-monsters/model.ts`
- Phaser rendering/input: `game.ts`
- Music/effects: `audio.ts`
- Touch UI and educational prompts: `GameClient.tsx`
- Optimized art/audio: `public/superelly/assets`, `public/superelly/audio`

Home Screen: open in iPhone Safari, Share > Add to Home Screen. A first online load is required. Runtime chunks are cached after fetching. New service worker versions replace the old asset cache. Physical iPhone performance, touch combinations and speaker behavior still require device testing.
